## Product overview

**AMBOSS GPT** brings AMBOSS medical knowledge into a ChatGPT conversation. Users can ask medical questions and receive responses that prioritize AMBOSS library content, with links to relevant AMBOSS articles for verification and further reading.

**Published value proposition:** trusted, up-to-date medical knowledge and quick reference to AMBOSS articles directly in ChatGPT.

## Intended use and users

AMBOSS presents the GPT as a convenient way to ask questions and consult AMBOSS medical knowledge. The product page positions the broader AMBOSS library for medical students and professionals.

The page does not define a specific user role, clinical workflow, or patient-care use case for AMBOSS GPT. Do not represent the GPT as a diagnostic or treatment service, or as a replacement for professional judgment, based on this source alone.

## Core capabilities

- Accepts natural-language questions in ChatGPT.
- Prioritizes AMBOSS library content when answering.
- Includes links to relevant AMBOSS articles with responses, so users can check sources and explore topics in more depth.
- Can be used in any language. AMBOSS says answers are automatically translated to the language being used when needed.
- May present suggested questions in the ChatGPT experience.

The AMBOSS page does not specify supported file types, image input, conversation memory, integrations beyond ChatGPT, model/provider version, or response-time guarantees.

## Access and activation

The activation instructions published by AMBOSS are:

1. Open [go.amboss.com/gpt](http://go.amboss.com/gpt).
2. In ChatGPT, open the **AMBOSS Medical Knowledge** menu and select **Keep in sidebar** to make it easier to return to.
3. Ask AMBOSS GPT a question or select a suggested question.
4. Authorize with an email address.

AMBOSS says the GPT is available to everyone at no additional charge. It recommends using the same email address as the user's AMBOSS account for the best experience.

### Prompt limits and membership

- Users who authorize with an email address other than their AMBOSS account email are limited to **50 prompts every 3 months**, according to the product FAQ.
- Using the user's AMBOSS account email lifts this AMBOSS prompt quota.
- OpenAI rate limits still apply and are outside AMBOSS's control.
- An AMBOSS membership is not described as a prerequisite for using the GPT. Members have unlimited access to relevant library articles; without an account, access to linked articles is limited.
- AMBOSS membership also includes other products and benefits, such as the medical library, Qbank, Anki integration, and Chrome integration. These are membership offerings, not necessarily features of AMBOSS GPT itself.

## Language and accuracy notes

AMBOSS describes its answers as intended to be up-to-date and evidence-based and says they prioritize trustworthy AMBOSS content. Answers include links to relevant AMBOSS articles.

AMBOSS cautions that translated answers may not fully reflect the original AMBOSS content and that errors are possible. For accuracy, users should consult the original information through the linked AMBOSS library articles.

Do not turn “prioritizes” or “designed for” into guarantees of correctness, completeness, or currency. The source page does not promise that every answer is fully sourced, error-free, or appropriate for a specific clinical decision.

## User benefits

- Ask a question in a conversational interface.
- Find AMBOSS reference material without first searching the library manually.
- Follow source links to verify an answer and read further.
- Use the tool in the user's preferred language, with the translation caveat above.
- Continue to relevant AMBOSS articles, with the breadth of access depending on AMBOSS account status.

## User journey

1. **Discover:** user follows the AMBOSS GPT link from AMBOSS's U.S. product page or opens the direct entry point.
2. **Set up:** user keeps AMBOSS Medical Knowledge in the ChatGPT sidebar if desired and authorizes via email.
3. **Ask:** user enters a medical question or chooses a suggestion.
4. **Review:** user reads the response and follows relevant AMBOSS article links to verify or deepen understanding.
5. **Continue:** user may sign in with an AMBOSS account for unlimited access to relevant articles and, when using the matching email, removal of the AMBOSS prompt quota.

## Messaging guidance

When describing AMBOSS GPT:

- Say it **brings AMBOSS medical knowledge into ChatGPT** or **prioritizes AMBOSS library content**.
- Highlight the linked AMBOSS articles as a way to verify and explore further.
- State the prompt quota and email condition together when discussing limits.
- Explain that OpenAI rate limits also apply.
- Include the translation caveat when promoting multilingual use.
- Distinguish the no-additional-charge GPT from the access limits on AMBOSS library articles.

Avoid claiming that the product:

- Guarantees accuracy, completeness, or real-time updates.
- Provides clinical decisions, diagnoses, or individualized treatment recommendations.
- Replaces clinicians or professional judgment.
- Has unlimited usage for all users.
- Is entirely free of any third-party platform requirements or limits.
