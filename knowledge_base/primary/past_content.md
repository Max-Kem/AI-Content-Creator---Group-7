### Key findings

Scientific achievements: AMBOSS communicates scientific results with confidence and credibility. The language emphasizes evidence, measurable outcomes, and the practical value of medical innovations.

Corporate milestones: Posts about acquisitions, investments, or organizational growth use an optimistic and forward-looking tone. AMBOSS presents its achievements as part of a broader mission to improve medical education and healthcare.

Company culture: Posts featuring employees, teams, or company achievements typically use a more personal and approachable tone. They emphasize collaboration, appreciation, and shared values.

## 6. Recommended KPI and Documentation Structure

For our project, the primary performance KPI should be:

`likes_count` – Number of likes received by an individual post.

For LinkedIn, total reactions can serve as a proxy when a breakdown of individual reaction types is unavailable. However, this distinction should be documented to avoid treating different metrics as identical.

Each selected post in our `past_content/` knowledge base should include:

- Post metadata: Platform, publication date, and original URL.
- Original content: The English text of the post.
- Engagement metrics: Likes/reactions, comments, and shares where available.
- Brand voice analysis: Tone, language style, and communication patterns.
- Key takeaways: What makes the post distinctive and what our AI system should learn from it.

## 7. Conclusion

The initial analysis suggests that AMBOSS uses several distinct communication styles, depending on the content category.

Scientific achievements and corporate milestones show particularly strong engagement among the posts researched. At the same time, company culture content provides valuable examples of a more approachable and human-centered brand voice.

For our AI Content Creator, this means that reproducing the AMBOSS brand voice requires more than simply using professional medical terminology. The system should also adapt its tone, structure, and messaging to the specific content type.

Our objective is to use these successful posts as reference material so that the AI can generate content that sounds authentic to AMBOSS rather than generic AI-generated marketing text.

Research limitation: The current findings are based on a small sample of publicly available LinkedIn posts. The ranking does not establish which AMBOSS posts performed best overall, and engagement figures alone do not demonstrate why a particular post was successful.
