## 2. Competitor overview

The market has two arenas, and AMBOSS is the only big player in both:
- **Exam prep (students):** UWorld, Lecturio
- **Clinical AI (doctors):** OpenEvidence, UpToDate Expert AI

### World: the exam prep leader
- **Scale:** 2M+ users (active and past); 95%+ of USMLE Step 1 test takers used it in 2021
- **Strength:** reputation as the gold standard; questions closely match the real exam style
- **Weakness:** most expensive major QBank; library costs extra
- **vs. AMBOSS:** AMBOSS is cheaper and bundles library, AI tools, Anki and clinical support, but has fewer questions

### Lecturio: the budget, video-first option
- **Offer:** 1,000+ free questions, no credit card
- **Strength:** lowest annual price of the major platforms; QBank built around video teaching
- **vs. AMBOSS:** better for video learners on a budget; AMBOSS is text-first with a deeper library and clinical tools

### OpenEvidence: the fast-growing clinical AI
- **Scale:** $12B valuation after a $250M Series D (Jan 2026); used by 40%+ of U.S. doctors across 10,000+ hospitals
- **Revenue:** $100M+ annually, less than a year after commercial launch
- **Model:** free for physicians, funded by advertising
- **How it works:** answers only from peer-reviewed literature and guidelines
- **vs. AMBOSS:** much bigger in U.S. clinical AI, but doctors only, no exam prep

### UpToDate Expert AI (Wolters Kluwer): the established giant
- **Scale:** UpToDate has 3M+ clinician users; Expert AI is in about 2,500 U.S. hospitals and health systems
- **Parent company:** €6.1B annual revenue (2025)
- **Strength:** answers only from expert-authored, peer-reviewed UpToDate content (7,600 expert clinicians)
- **Extra:** clinicians earn CME credit while using the AI
- **vs. AMBOSS:** strong in hospitals and enterprise, but expensive and not built for students

## 4. AMBOSS USPs

1. **One companion for the whole career:** preclinical study, exam prep and clinical practice in one platform.
2. **Best value bundle:** QBank, library, AI tools, Anki and clinical support in one subscription, cheaper than UWorld.
3. **Measurable results (company claims):** users score 10.4 points higher on Step 2 CK; 95% of residents say it helps them be better doctors.
4. **AI safety (company claim):** ranked #1 in a Stanford-Harvard clinical AI study, ahead of OpenEvidence, Doximity and Glass Health.
5. **Made by physicians:** founded by doctors, 150+ physicians on staff.
6. **Trusted by top institutions:** including Yale, NYU, UW Medicine and Icahn (Mount Sinai).

## 5. AMBOSS GPT vs. competitors

| Price | Free for everyone | Free for verified physicians | Paid subscription | Free / paid |
| Source of answers | AMBOSS library | Peer-reviewed literature | UpToDate content | General training data |
| Who it's for | Students + doctors | Doctors | Hospital clinicians | Everyone |

**Key message:** AMBOSS GPT makes the ChatGPT you already use reliable. It is free, works in any language, and grounds answers in the AMBOSS library.

## 6. Takeaways for content

- **Lead with trust:** "reliable answers, no second-guessing". Plain ChatGPT is the real everyday competitor.
- **Use "free" as a hook.**
- **Speak to students and doctors**, the audience no single competitor covers both of.
- **Label company claims** (10.4 points, #1 study) as AMBOSS data, not independent facts.
